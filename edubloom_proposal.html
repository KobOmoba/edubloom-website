Solution 2 — For the Proprietor

The only school software with a Proprietor-only fraud audit.

Edu-BLOOM is built on a principle no competitor uses: the bank statement is the only thing that can mark a student as paid. No Bursar approval. No manual confirmation. No single point of trust between the parent's bank and the student's record.

How it works
When a parent pays by bank transfer, they tap a link in their WhatsApp reminder and upload a photo of their debit alert. The school sees the receipt. But the student's fee is only marked as paid when the Bursar uploads the bank statement CSV — and the system finds a matching transaction automatically. No human approval is possible. The bank is the witness.
POS payments — with receipt numbers
When a parent pays at the school POS machine, the Bursar records the payment with the RRN (Receipt Reference Number) printed on the POS slip. This number appears on your bank statement and on your terminal's daily settlement report. The Proprietor can cross-check every POS entry against those two records at any time. A Bursar who invents a POS payment has to invent an RRN — and it will not exist on the bank statement.
🔒 The Proprietor Audit Log — only you can see this
Every fee change of any kind — bank statement match, POS entry, manual recording, payment deletion, or edit — is written permanently to a log that only the Proprietor's account can open. Every entry shows: the staff member's name, their role, the exact time and date, the amount, and the method. Any entry from the last 7 days can be reversed with one tap if something does not look right. No other school software in Nigeria does this.
Solution 3 — For Parents

Pay. Confirm. Done. From their phone.

Every term, Edu-BLOOM sends WhatsApp fee reminders to parents showing their child's outstanding balance and the school's bank account details. The message includes a payment confirmation link.

When the parent taps the link, they see a page showing the amount due, the bank account to pay to, and a button: "I Have Paid — Upload Receipt." They upload a photo of their bank debit alert. The school sees it instantly in the receipts queue. When the bank statement is uploaded and the transaction matches, the student's record updates automatically. The parent is never turned back at the gate for a payment that was already made.

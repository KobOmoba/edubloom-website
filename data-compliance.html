Data & Compliance

Effective date: 5 July 2026 · AariNAT Company Limited (RC-1732521)
This page sets out, in more technical detail than our Privacy Policy, how AariNAT complies with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 ("NDPA") across the Educational Bloom platform. It is intended for school administrators, auditors, and regulators.

1. Regulatory Framework

  • Law: Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023
  • Regulator: Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) — ndpc.gov.ng
  • Data Controller (platform-level): AariNAT Company Limited, RC-1732521
  • Data Controller (student/staff-level): the individual subscribing school
  • Data Processor: AariNAT Company Limited, processing on each school's instructions

2. NDPC Registration

Organisations processing personal data of more than the NDPC's prescribed threshold of data subjects, or processing certain categories of sensitive data, are required to register with the NDPC as a Data Controller or Processor of major importance. AariNAT tracks its school and student count against this threshold and will complete NDPC registration before scaling past its early-stage rollout, as reflected in our internal roadmap.

4. Categories of Personal Data Processed

  • Ordinary personal data: names, phone numbers, emails, class/role, attendance, academic scores, fee balances.
  • Sensitive personal data: we do not intentionally collect health, biometric, or genetic data. Any health-related notes a school chooses to enter (e.g. allergy notes in the Health module) are entered and controlled solely by the school, and schools are responsible for the lawful basis for capturing that information.

5. Children's Data — Special Note

Most data subjects in the Student module are minors. In line with NDPA requirements around children's data:

  • Schools (not AariNAT) collect student data directly from parents/guardians as part of enrolment and are responsible for parental/guardian consent or another valid lawful basis;
  • AariNAT does not use student data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing the school management service instructed by the school;
  • WhatsApp messages to parents (fee reminders, absence alerts) are triggered by the school, using contact details the school itself has collected.

6. Cross-Border Data Transfer

Data is hosted on Google Firebase, which may process data on servers outside Nigeria. The NDPA permits cross-border transfer where the recipient country/organisation provides an adequate level of protection, or where appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) are in place. Google maintains globally recognised security and privacy certifications and standard data-processing terms that we rely on to meet this requirement. We continue to monitor NDPC guidance on approved transfer mechanisms as the regulatory framework matures.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Active-subscription data is retained for the life of the subscription. Following termination, data is retained for 24 months for account-recovery and record-keeping purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Schools or individuals may request earlier deletion, subject to any legal retention obligation.

8. Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach involving personal data likely to result in risk to affected individuals, AariNAT will notify the NDPC within the timeframe required by the NDPA and inform affected schools without undue delay, so schools can in turn notify affected parents/guardians as appropriate.

9. Data Protection Impact & Accountability

As AariNAT scales (per our staged rollout plan), we will conduct data protection impact assessments for new features that materially increase data-processing risk (e.g. biometric attendance, expanded payment integrations) before launch.

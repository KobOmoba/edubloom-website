3. Children's Data

Educational Bloom processes personal data belonging to students under 18, entered by their school. Under the NDPA, processing a child's data requires a lawful basis and, where relying on consent, verifiable consent from a parent or guardian. Because schools — not AariNAT — collect this data directly from parents/guardians as part of enrolment, the school is responsible for obtaining that consent or establishing another lawful basis (e.g. performance of the school's educational contract with the parent) before entering a child's data into the platform. AariNAT processes that data only on the school's instructions and does not use student data for advertising or profiling.

4. Why We Process This Data (Lawful Basis)

  • Contract — to provide the school management service the school has subscribed to.
  • Legitimate interest — to run agent commissions, prevent fraud, and improve the platform.
  • Consent — for optional features such as WhatsApp fee reminders to parents.
  • Legal obligation — where required by NDPC or other Nigerian regulators.

5. How We Store and Protect Data

  • Data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database provider. Data may be processed on servers outside Nigeria; Google maintains internationally recognised security certifications, and we require it to handle data under contractual data-protection terms consistent with NDPA cross-border transfer requirements.
  • Access to admin data (agents, deals, ledger) is restricted to AariNAT staff who need it.
  • School staff access is role-based — a Class Teacher, for example, cannot see fee data.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.

6. Retention

We retain personal data for as long as a school's subscription is active, and for 24 months after a subscription ends, to allow for account recovery, dispute resolution, and legal/regulatory record-keeping. After this period, data is deleted or anonymised on request.

7. Sharing of Data

We share data only with:

  • The school itself (for its own students/staff);
  • Payment processors (e.g. Kora/Korapay) strictly to process fee payments;
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (Google Firebase) as our data processor;
  • Regulators or law enforcement, where legally required.

8. Your Rights Under the NDPA

Data subjects (parents, guardians, staff, agents) have the right to:

  • Request access to personal data held about them or their child;
  • Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Request deletion of data, subject to lawful retention needs;
  • Object to certain processing (e.g. marketing messages);
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

To exercise any of these rights, contact your school directly (for student/staff data) or AariNAT at the email above.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform grows. Material changes will be communicated to schools via WhatsApp or in-app notice.

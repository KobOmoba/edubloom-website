For Proprietors & Proprietresses

Your fees. Your ledger.
Your audit trail.

Every school owner has wondered, at some point, whether every naira collected actually made it to the school account. Edu-BLOOM is the only school software built to answer that question with certainty.

Rule 1
Bank statement is the ONLY authority
No Bursar can manually mark a student as paid from a receipt. Only when the bank statement CSV is uploaded and a transaction matches does the fee update. One source of truth. No exceptions.
Rule 2
Every POS payment requires a receipt number
Parents who pay at the school POS machine — their transaction RRN is recorded alongside the payment. The Proprietor can cross-check every entry against the terminal's daily settlement report at any time.
Rule 3
Proprietor audit log — invisible to all staff
Every fee change of any kind — bank match, manual entry, deletion, edit — is logged permanently with the name of the staff member, timestamp, and amount. Only the Proprietor can see this log. Any entry can be reversed within 7 days with one tap.
